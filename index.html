About This Pack

ZCraft is a curated collection of quality-of-life, exploration, tech, and world-generation mods built on Forge. The pack blends deep tech progression from Mekanism and Industrial Foregoing with expanded exploration via The Aether, Alex's Mobs, and YUNG's world-gen suite — all backed by performance mods like Sodium, Lithium, and Ferrite Core to keep things running smoothly.

Whether you're here to check if a mod is on the server, find its official wiki, or troubleshoot your client install, you're in the right place.

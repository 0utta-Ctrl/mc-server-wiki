Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the modpack, installation, and gameplay. Click a question to expand the answer.

First, check that your Minecraft and Forge versions exactly match the pack's requirements. Most crashes come from mismatched versions, missing dependencies, or insufficient allocated RAM (see the Installation Guide). Check the crash log in your instance's logs folder and share it in the support channel if you're stuck.

We recommend at least 4–6 GB. With 139 mods loaded, less than 4 GB will likely cause slow loading or out-of-memory crashes.

Purely client-side cosmetic or QoL mods (like extra shaders or HUD tweaks) are usually fine. Avoid adding mods that alter world generation, add new blocks/items, or change gameplay mechanics, as this can cause desyncs or crashes on a shared server.

Visit the Mod List page and click through to the mod's Modrinth or CurseForge page. Many mods also include in-game documentation via Patchouli guidebooks — check your inventory after joining.

Try lowering render distance, disabling Distant Horizons temporarily, and verifying that Iris-based shaders aren't enabled if your GPU is older. Also confirm Ferrite Core and ModernFix are active — both help reduce memory overhead.
